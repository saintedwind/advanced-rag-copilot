# Northstar Cloud Platform — Service Levels

## Availability commitments
Northstar Cloud publishes monthly service availability measured across the customer-facing control plane and managed data plane. The Standard tier has a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment, while the Business tier guarantees 99.95%. The Mission Critical tier guarantees 99.99% monthly uptime. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance is excluded from the calculation.

## Service credits
If monthly availability falls below a tier commitment, customers may request service credits within 30 days of the incident. Standard credits are 10% of the affected monthly service fee for availability between 99.0% and the commitment, and 25% below 99.0%. Business customers receive 15% and 30% credits in equivalent bands. Credits are applied to future invoices and are not cash refunds.

## Support response targets
Standard support responds to critical incidents within four hours. Business support has a one-hour critical response target. Mission Critical customers receive a 15-minute response target and a named incident commander. Response targets are not resolution-time guarantees.
